How it works

  1. Step 1

    Get Loaf

    Copy the starter and run it. Your site opens in the browser.

    bunx degit anas1412/loaf my-site
cd my-site
bun dev

  2. Step 2

    Add a page

    Create a file in public/. The header and footer are added for you.

    <title>About me</title>

<main class="p-8">
  <h1>Hi, I'm Sam</h1>
</main>

  3. Step 3

    Save things

    Add collection('notes') and your form remembers what people type.

    <main x-data="collection('notes')">
  <form @submit.prevent="add($el)">
    <input name="text">
    <button>Save</button>
  </form>
</main>

Why Loaf

  • Nothing to set up

    No accounts and no settings. Copy it and it runs.

  • Save and refresh

    Change a file, refresh the browser, see it. That's the whole routine.

  • It remembers

    Forms save what people type, and it's still there tomorrow.

  • Looks good already

    Ready-made buttons, cards and menus, and 35 themes to pick from.
